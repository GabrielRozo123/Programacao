==============================================================
  SIMULACAO DEM - PENEIRA VIBRATORIA HAVER & BOECKER
  Simcenter STAR-CCM+  |  Meshfree DEM
  Baseado nos tutoriais PDF fornecidos (excavator shovel DEM)
==============================================================

ARQUIVOS DE GEOMETRIA
  Screen.step           -> Solid Region  (peca solida)
  Domain.step           -> Particle Region (dominio de particulas)

DIMENSOES DO MODELO
  Deck da peneira (interno): 424 x 634 mm
  Frame externo            : 448 x 658 mm   altura: 190 mm
  Abertura (square mesh)   : 38 x 38 mm     barra: 4 mm   passo: 42 mm
  Grade                    : 10 x 15 = 150 aberturas
  Dominio (Particle Region): 458 x 640 x 668 mm (Larg x Alt x Comp)
    - acima do deck: 400 mm
    - abaixo do deck: 240 mm (inclui frame + 50 mm coleta)

SISTEMA DE COORDENADAS
  X : largura da peneira
  Y : vertical (+Y para cima, gravidade = -Y)
  Z : comprimento (alimentacao -> descarga)
  Topo do deck em Y = 0

----------------------------------------------------------------
PASSO 1 – IMPORTAR GEOMETRIA
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. File > Import > CAD Model
2. Importar Screen.step
3. Importar Domain.step
   (ou importar HB_VibScreen_DEM.step de uma vez)
4. Verificar no arvore: Geometry > Parts > Screen e Domain

----------------------------------------------------------------
PASSO 2 – MODELOS FISICOS (conforme PDF "Selecting Physics Models")
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Clicar com botao direito em Continua > New Physics Continuum
2. Selecionar na ordem:

   Grupo                   | Modelo
   ------------------------|----------------------------------------
   Optional Models         | Meshfree DEM
   (automaticos)           | Lagrangian Multiphase
                           | Multiphase Interaction
                           | Discrete Element Model (DEM)
                           | Solution Interpolation
   Time                    | Implicit Unsteady  (automatico)
   Optional Models         | Gravity

----------------------------------------------------------------
PASSO 3 – PARTICULAS E INTERACOES (conforme PDF "Defining DEM Particles")
----------------------------------------------------------------
Duas fases de particulas (oversize + undersize para classificacao):

3.1 Fase 1 – Particulas Oversize (retidas na peneira)
    - Lagrangian Multiphase > Lagrangian Phases > New > Free-stream phase
    - Renomear: Particles_Oversize
    - Particle Type    : DEM Particles
    - Particle Shape   : Spherical Particles
    - Material         : Solid -> renomear "Rubber" para "Rock"
    - Young's Modulus  : 10.0 MPa  (Constant)

3.2 Fase 2 – Particulas Undersize (passam pelas aberturas)
    - Idem acima; renomear: Particles_Undersize
    - Young's Modulus  : 10.0 MPa  (mesma rocha)

3.3 Interacoes (Hertz Mindlin)
    Criar as seguintes Phase Interactions:

    a) Particles-Particles (entre quaisquer particulas)
       - DEM Contact Model: Hertz Mindlin
       - Static Friction Coefficient          : 0.9
       - Normal Restitution Coefficient       : 0.2
       - Tangential Restitution Coefficient   : 0.2
       - Rolling Resistance Method            : Constant Torque
       - Coefficient of Rolling Resistance    : 0.5

    b) Particles-Wall (particulas com a peneira/frame)
       - DEM Contact Model: Hertz Mindlin
       - Static Friction Coefficient          : 0.61
       - Normal Restitution Coefficient       : 0.2
       - Tangential Restitution Coefficient   : 0.2
       - Rolling Resistance Method            : Constant Torque
       - Coefficient of Rolling Resistance    : 0.1

----------------------------------------------------------------
PASSO 4 – ATRIBUIR REGIOES (conforme PDF "Assigning Particle and Solid Regions")
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Geometry > Parts > selecionar Screen e Domain (multi-select)
2. Botao direito > Assign Parts to Regions
   - Create a Region for Each Part
   - Create a Boundary for Each Part Surface
   - Do Not Create Interfaces From Contacts
3. Regions > Screen > Type : Solid Region
4. Regions > Domain > Type : Particle Region

----------------------------------------------------------------
PASSO 5 – CONDICOES DE CONTORNO DO DOMINIO
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Paredes laterais e superior (Domain boundaries):
    Type : Wall  (particulas oversize quicam e ficam na peneira)
- Parede inferior do Domain (abaixo da peneira):
    Type : Escape  (contabiliza particulas undersize que passaram)

----------------------------------------------------------------
PASSO 6 – VOLUME DE INJECAO E INJECTOR
(conforme PDF "Injecting the Particles")
----------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Criar volume de injecao (Injection Volume):
    - Geometry > Parts > New Shape Part > Block
    - Corner 1 / Corner 2 (coordenadas em metros):

      Variavel | Corner 1  | Corner 2
      ---------|-----------|----------
      X        | -0.212 m  | +0.212 m   (toda a largura interna)
      Y        | +0.050 m  | +0.350 m   (entre 50 mm e 350 mm acima do deck)
      Z        | -0.317 m  | -0.120 m   (primeiro terco da peneira = zona de alimentacao)

    - Renomear Block para "Injection_Volume"

6.2 Injector – Particles_Oversize (d = 50 mm)
    - Injectors > New
    - Type              : Random Injector
    - Lagrangian Phase  : Particles_Oversize
    - Inputs            : Parts > Injection_Volume
    Condicoes:
    - Particle Packing Specification > Method : Parcel Count
    - Number of Parcels : 1000
    - Number of Seeds   : 250
    - Particle Diameter : 50 mm

6.3 Injector – Particles_Undersize (d = 25 mm)
    - Idem acima
    - Lagrangian Phase  : Particles_Undersize
    - Number of Parcels : 1000
    - Number of Seeds   : 250
    - Particle Diameter : 25 mm

    Resultado: 2000 particulas totais (1000 oversize + 1000 undersize)
    Particulas de 25 mm passarao pelas aberturas de 38 mm.
    Particulas de 50 mm serao retidas na peneira.

----------------------------------------------------------------
PASSO 7 – MOVIMENTO DA PENEIRA (adaptar "Prescribing the Bucket Motion")
----------------------------------------------------------------
A peneira vibra com movimento linear a 15 Hz, amplitude 5 mm,
direcao a 45 deg da horizontal (tipico peneira linear H&B).

7.1 Tools > Motions > New > Translation
    Renomear: "Screen_Motion"
    Translation Velocity: [0.0, ${screenVy}, ${screenVz}]

7.2 Superposing Motions > New > Rotation  (opcional para movimento eliptico)
    Rotation Rate  : ${screenRotation}
    Rotation Axis  : [1.0, 0.0, 0.0]
    Origin         : [0.0, 0.0, 0.0] m

7.3 Field Functions (criar em Tools > Field Functions):

    screenVy   = 0.0   [m/s]     (sem componente vertical na vibracao linear)

    screenVz   = A * (2*PI*f) * cos(2*PI*f * $Time)
               onde A = 0.005 m (amplitude), f = 15 Hz
               = 0.4712 * cos(94.25 * $Time)

    screenRotation = 0.0  (vibracao puramente translacional)

    Para inclinacao equivalente a 15 deg: usar componente em Y tambem
    screenVy   = A * (2*PI*f) * sin(rad(15)) * cos(2*PI*f * $Time)
    screenVz   = A * (2*PI*f) * cos(rad(15)) * cos(2*PI*f * $Time)

7.4 Regions > Screen > Physics Values > Motion Specification
    Motion: Screen_Motion -> Translation

NOTA SOBRE INCLINACAO:
    A geometria foi criada horizontal. Para simular inclinacao de 15 deg
    da peneira Haver & Boecker, usar uma das opcoes:
    (a) Ajustar vetor de gravidade:
        Gravity = [0.0, -9.81*cos(15 deg), -9.81*sin(15 deg)]
                = [0.0,  -9.479,  -2.539]  m/s^2
    (b) Ou girar ambos Screen e Domain 15 deg em torno de X ao importar.

----------------------------------------------------------------
PASSO 8 – CONFIGURACAO DO TEMPO
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Stopping Criteria > Maximum Physical Time : 5.0 s
- Time Step                                 : 1e-4 s
  (DEM recomenda dt < 20% do tempo de contato de Hertz)

ESTIMATIVA DO TIME STEP CRITICO (Hertz):
  tc = pi * sqrt(m / k_eff) ; k_eff ~ E* * r_eff
  Para particulas de 50 mm e E = 10 MPa:
  dt_critico ~ 1 × 10^-4 a 5 × 10^-4 s  → usar dt = 1e-4 s (conservador)

----------------------------------------------------------------
PASSO 9 – MONITORAR / POS-PROCESSAMENTO
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Reports > DEM: Escape Rate (conta particulas que saem pelo fundo = undersize)
- Scene > Particles coloridas por Diameter (distingue oversize/undersize)
- Scene > Screen colorida por Pressure (forcas de contato)
- Reports > Mass Flow Rate na fronteira "Escape" do Domain
